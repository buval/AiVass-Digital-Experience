## Rule
For reflective Field Notes, prefer a restrained emotional photograph with the core visual framework present subtly in the scene or as a secondary panel. Keep branded cover artwork for downloadable PDFs and social previews rather than using it as an oversized reading-page banner.

**Why:** The user found the large branded Support System banner visually dominant and disconnected from the article. A human moment plus a quieter wheel made the page feel more emotionally credible and brought readers to the article sooner.

**How to apply:** Keep article heroes contained within equal page margins, use shorter editorial proportions than a full cover, align them to the reading grid, and preserve every visible face at all responsive crops.
