Madame, **do the Founder Story before launch.** The current About page can explain *what AiVass is*, but without your story it still misses **why AiVass had to exist**. For a boutique premium studio, the founder is part of the trust architecture.

I would not make it long. Add one strong founder section, ideally with a sophisticated portrait of you, then keep the rest of the Architectural Transformation page.

### Founder Story — recommended copy

**Built from transformation, not theory.**

AiVass grew from my own experience of transformation.

For more than 17 years, I worked across marketing, strategy, program management and technology, helping global organizations turn complex ambitions into systems that could actually work. At Adobe, Intuit and across different industries, I learned how to bring structure to ambiguity, connect people around a shared direction, and move ideas from possibility into execution.

But the most important transformation was personal.

I rebuilt my career across countries, navigated my own extraordinary-ability journey, moved through uncertainty, reinvention and identity shifts, and learned that meaningful change rarely begins with doing more. It begins with seeing differently — understanding who you are becoming, what matters, what proves your value, and what architecture can carry that future forward.

That realization became AiVass.

Today, I am building the kind of company I wanted to find myself: one where **intelligence and humanity belong together**, where technology expands human potential rather than replacing it, and where transformation creates capability that remains after the intervention ends.

**Building Extraordinary Futures** is therefore more than a tagline.

It is the work.

*— Maria Boukhval, Founder of AiVass*

### Then transition directly into the brand philosophy

> **Automation with Soul.**
> The future should become more intelligent without becoming less human.

Then:

**Purpose · Proof · Permanence**

This creates a much stronger narrative:

**Maria → lived transformation → AiVass → philosophy → methodology → solutions.**

That is also much more compelling for your Replit competition story because the redesign stops being *“I changed my website.”* It becomes:

> **I redesigned the digital expression of a company that grew out of my own transformation — and used Replit to turn that philosophy into an interactive system.**

So my recommendation is: **do not launch the About page as-is. Add this one Founder Story section first, then finalize.** No additional biography or résumé timeline is necessary right now.
