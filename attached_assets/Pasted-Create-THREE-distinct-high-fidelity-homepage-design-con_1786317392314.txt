THE THREE DESIGN CONCEPTS

CONCEPT A — CONSTELLATION / CONVERGENCE

Create a very spacious, editorial hero where three separated star nodes move conceptually toward the center and form the Triad.

Use subtle constellation / orbital geometry, but keep it sophisticated and restrained.

The atmosphere should feel like intelligence, possibility and connection.

CONCEPT B — ARCHITECTURAL TRANSFORMATION

Create a highly architectural experience where three visual fields / planes / openings gradually align into the Triad.

Use cream architectural space, horizon light, subtle depth, portals and strong editorial typography.

This should feel like a premium transformation studio and be the most visually luxurious of the three, without becoming decorative luxury.

CONCEPT C — PURPOSE / PROOF / PERMANENCE

Create an intellectual, strategic interpretation of the Triad.

Each point represents:

PURPOSE
What should transform and why?

PROOF
How is transformation made visible?

PERMANENCE
What remains after the transformation?

The three ideas visually assemble into one AiVass system.

This concept should feel the most strategic and ownable.
